# Explore Ba Na Hills During the April 30 – May 1 Holiday
###### 09/04/2026
Share
Add to Favorites
Print
> _During the Hung Kings’ Commemoration Day and the April 30 – May 1 holiday, Da Nang – one of Vietnam’s most livable cities – stands out as a truly worthwhile choice for a joyful and fulfilling getaway. Beyond its diverse tourism ecosystem, this coastal city offers fresh and vibrant experiences, especially atop the legendary Ba Na Hills._
## ****_**When Light Is Not Just Seen, But “Touched”**_****
If you thought you had already experienced everything Sun World Ba Na Hills has to offer, the Interactive Light Art space, launched in mid-April, will make you think again. This is a completely new art installation – making its debut on Nui Chua – created by Chọn Art with the special theme “Origin.”
![](https://danangfantasticity.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/kham-pha-ba-na-hills-trong-ky-nghi-le-30-4-1-5-04-1024x683.jpg)
__The “Origin” Interactive Light Art space makes its debut at Ba Na Hills.__
Located at the former Wax Museum site, the Interactive Light Art space spans 800m². Yet once inside, it feels like stepping into another universe—where the boundary between reality and illusion nearly disappears. What makes this space truly unique is its optical sensor technology: every step, every gesture you make transforms the environment in real time. Light is no longer static—it reacts, interacts, and “communicates” with you.
A journey across seven zones takes visitors from the “Eternal Light Ring” beneath the ocean, into the magical world of “Seven-Colored Jellyfish,” then to the “Hundred Eggs” installation, before concluding at the “Nirvana” zone—leaving behind lasting impressions. This unique experience is priced at just VND 100,000 during the trial phase—well worth it for a completely new experience and a memorable holiday moment.
![](https://danangfantasticity.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/kham-pha-ba-na-hills-trong-ky-nghi-le-30-4-1-5-08-1024x683.jpg)
__The glowing giant “Hundred Eggs” installation in the interactive art space.__
## ****_**_**Sun Festival – A Spectrum of Emotions**_**_****
Summer is the most beautiful time of the year at Ba Na Hills, when nature and scenery blend into a vibrant setting. It is also when the “heart” of this land – the Sun Kingdom – comes alive with the return of the Ba Na Sun Fest. More than just an event, it is a journey of emotions, from joy to excitement, taking place from day to night with highlights including performances, culinary experiences, and premium craft beer bearing the Ba Na signature. The festival also opens the gateway for visitors to explore four themed realms, meet mythical gods, and fully immerse themselves in the fairy-tale kingdom of Ba Na this summer.
![](https://danangfantasticity.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/kham-pha-ba-na-hills-trong-ky-nghi-le-30-4-1-5-05-1024x683.jpg)
_The Sun Festival brings a festive atmosphere with entertainment activities for families and friends._
In addition to captivating mini art shows throughout the day, visitors can also enjoy two signature performances “tailor-made” for Ba Na: the thrilling cabaret show _“After Glow”_ and the romantic musical performance _“Love in the Sky.”_
For food lovers, dozens of high-end restaurants and hundreds of street food stalls offering a wide range of cuisines—from local specialties to international dishes—will surely delight your taste buds.
![](https://danangfantasticity.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/kham-pha-ba-na-hills-trong-ky-nghi-le-30-4-1-5-01-1024x683.jpg)
__Spectacular visual impressions from outdoor performances._  
_ During this period, the resort is offering a special dining voucher worth VND 300,000, discounted to VND 250,000 when purchased with the cable car combo: VND 1,250,000 for adults and VND 1,050,000 for children (1m–1.4m) and seniors.
## ****_Thrilling Games and a Blooming Floral Wonderland_****
If festivals and art nourish the soul, then Fantasy Park is where you can release all your energy with exhilarating games. Among them, the outdoor alpine coaster stands out as a truly “next-level” experience.
Imagine sitting on a sled, controlling speeds of up to 40 km/h, racing through dramatic curves and double loops at an altitude of nearly 1,500 meters above sea level. Below lies a sea of clouds, beside you stand ancient castles—creating a breathtaking scene that will make you want to relive the experience again and again. Beyond the coaster, Fantasy Park offers a diverse and exciting world of games that will keep you entertained for hours.
![](https://danangfantasticity.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/kham-pha-ba-na-hills-trong-ky-nghi-le-30-4-1-5-02-1024x683.jpg)
__Experience the thrilling alpine coaster across the valley.__
Tech enthusiasts should not miss the 4D cinema _“Moon Intersection”_ with 331 seats, offering a multi-dimensional experience in just 10 minutes; or the VR-powered _“Flying Eye” Airship_ , allowing you to travel across deserts, galaxies, and iconic landmarks without leaving the country. All experiences are free and located within the Moon Castle.
During this time, thousands of roses at Rosa Garden bloom in full splendor, transforming the French Village into a “mini Paris” with cinematic photo spots. Of course, no trip is complete without check-in moments at the hydrangea garden or iconic landmarks such as the Golden Bridge, Moon Castle, and Sun God Waterfall.
![](https://danangfantasticity.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/kham-pha-ba-na-hills-trong-ky-nghi-le-30-4-1-5-07-1024x683.jpg)
_Rosa Garden in full bloom – a must-visit check-in spot this holiday_
## ****_Fly with Sun PhuQuoc Airways – Unlock Exclusive Privileges_****
Smart travel choices can make your holiday even more complete. That’s why more and more savvy travelers are choosing Sun PhuQuoc Airways (SPA) as their companion for this Da Nang holiday.
With the “One Ticket – A Million Joys” program, passengers flying with SPA receive a WowPass Silver worth VND 300,000. This “privilege pass” grants priority access at Sun World Ba Na Hills, allowing you to enjoy attractions without waiting in line. In addition, SPA tickets offer 10–15% discounts within Sun Group’s luxury hospitality ecosystem in Da Nang, including Mercure Danang French Village Bana Hills, Novotel Danang Premier Han River, as well as complimentary green fees and ProShop discounts at Ba Na Hills Golf Club.
![](https://danangfantasticity.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/kham-pha-ba-na-hills-trong-ky-nghi-le-30-4-1-5-03-1024x683.jpg)
__The timeless and romantic charm of the French Village creates a unique resort experience.__
This April 30 – May 1 holiday, Da Nang promises a vibrant and emotional journey, filled with diverse experiences that will leave lasting memories.
