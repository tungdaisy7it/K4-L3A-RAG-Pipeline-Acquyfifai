# Touch Tet moments by the Han River – Dragon Bridge and the springtime river show 2026
###### 04/02/2026
Share
Add to Favorites
Print
> Every Tet season, Da Nang reveals its own distinctive moments, where light, movement, and emotion converge on the surface of the Han River.
In the journey of returning to origin, Tet in Da Nang is not merely a time of rest, but an opportunity for visitors to feel the city’s rhythm through familiar symbols—vivid, dynamic, and rich in emotion.
![Touch Tet moments by the Han River](https://danangfantasticity.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/cau-rong-phun-nuoc-phun-lua-trong-cac-dem-dien-ra-dai-hoi-the-thao-hoc-sinh-dong-nam-a-lan-thu-13-1.jpg)
**Dragon Bridge – An emotional touchpoint of radiant spring nights**
Amid the festive atmosphere of **Binh Ngo Spring 2026** , Dragon Bridge remains an unmissable rendezvous by the Han River. The fire-breathing and water-spraying performances take place on the evenings from **13–22 February 2026** (from the 26th day of the last lunar month of **At Ty** to the 6th day of the first lunar month of **Binh Ngo**), creating a series of high-energy visual moments in the heart of the coastal city.
On **New Year’s Eve, 16 February 2026** (the 29th day of the last lunar month), Dragon Bridge performs a **water display at midnight (24:00)** —a delicate transition that closes the old year and welcomes the new one in a gentle, reflective, and hopeful atmosphere. As jets of water rise and lights shimmer across the river’s surface, the moment becomes a distinctly Da Nang Tet touchpoint, where the warmth of reunion and the promise of new beginnings quietly unfold.
**Han River Bridge – A slower movement for deeper emotion**
Alongside this, the nights when **Han River Bridge** opens for river traffic always offer a very different feeling. In the calm of spring nights, the bridge slowly rotating feels like a pause in time—allowing visitors to touch Da Nang in a more contemplative, deeper way.
The bridge opening schedule includes the evenings of:
  * **14–15 February 2026** (27th–28th day of the last lunar month of At Ty)
  * **17–18 February 2026** (1st–2nd day of the first lunar month of Binh Ngo)
  * **21–22 February 2026** (5th–6th day of the first lunar month of Binh Ngo)

![Touch Tet moments by the Han River 2](https://danangfantasticity.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Song-Han-sang-ruc-trong-nhung-dem-phao-hoa-DIFF-2023.jpg)
Amid the riverside lights and the slower rhythm of the early days of the year, these are moments that invite visitors to pause, take a deep breath of spring air, and feel a Da Nang that is real, authentic, and close to life.
Come and experience Da Nang—where original values are gently awakened, and where every moment by the Han River can become a cherished memory. A city vibrant enough to stir emotions, serene enough to touch deeply, and refined enough to make you want to stay longer—both in your journey and in your memories.
