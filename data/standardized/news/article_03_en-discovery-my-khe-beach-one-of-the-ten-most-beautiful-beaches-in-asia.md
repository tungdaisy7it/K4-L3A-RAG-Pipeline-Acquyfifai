# My Khe Beach: one of the ten most beautiful beaches in Asia
###### 02/04/2024
Share
Add to Favorites
Print
_My Khe Beach, located in the Son Tra district, has been proudly ranked Asia’s 6th most beautiful beach by the renowned global travel review website Tripadvisor. This significant recognition was given as part of the Travelers’ Choice Awards Best of the Best 2024. The beach is a major attraction for domestic and foreign tourists, who flock to this beautiful destination on sunny days to enjoy the sea, sand, and invigorating fresh air._
![](https://danangfantasticity.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/chiem-nguong-bien-my-khe-1-trong-10-bai-bien-dep-nhat-chau-a-01.jpg)
_My Khe Beach is a 900-meter-long stretch parallel to the Hoang Sa-Vo Nguyen Giap tourist route._
![](https://danangfantasticity.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/chiem-nguong-bien-my-khe-1-trong-10-bai-bien-dep-nhat-chau-a-02.jpg)
_My Khe Beach has countless positive reviews worldwide and is rated 4.5/5 stars on Tripadvisor, a global travel review platform._
![](https://danangfantasticity.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/chiem-nguong-bien-my-khe-1-trong-10-bai-bien-dep-nhat-chau-a-03.jpg)
_My Khe Beach is on the same route as Son Tra peninsula and Ngu Hanh Son landscape, two other popular tourist destinations._
![](https://danangfantasticity.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/chiem-nguong-bien-my-khe-1-trong-10-bai-bien-dep-nhat-chau-a-04.jpg)
_My Khe Beach is a popular destination among international tourists to relax, have fun, and enjoy the sea breeze._
![](https://danangfantasticity.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/chiem-nguong-bien-my-khe-1-trong-10-bai-bien-dep-nhat-chau-a-05.jpg)
_Tripadvisor rates My Khe Beach as a must-visit destination due to its soft sand, gentle waves, swaying coconut trees, and designated swimming and surfing areas._
![](https://danangfantasticity.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/chiem-nguong-bien-my-khe-1-trong-10-bai-bien-dep-nhat-chau-a-06.jpg)
_Da Nang is home to the stunning My Khe Beach, considered one of the area’s most beautiful natural beaches._
![](https://danangfantasticity.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/chiem-nguong-bien-my-khe-1-trong-10-bai-bien-dep-nhat-chau-a-07.jpg)
_Coastal stalls offering soft drinks and fast food are popular among tourists visiting My Khe beach._
![](https://danangfantasticity.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/chiem-nguong-bien-my-khe-1-trong-10-bai-bien-dep-nhat-chau-a-08.jpg)
_Tourists can capture stunning photos at one of Asia’s top ten beautiful beaches._
![](https://danangfantasticity.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/chiem-nguong-bien-my-khe-1-trong-10-bai-bien-dep-nhat-chau-a-09.jpg)
![](https://danangfantasticity.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/chiem-nguong-bien-my-khe-1-trong-10-bai-bien-dep-nhat-chau-a-010.jpg)
_My Khe Beach is known for its long stretch of fine, white sand and gentle, clear blue waves, making it a perfect destination for tourists to swim and relax._
![](https://danangfantasticity.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/chiem-nguong-bien-my-khe-1-trong-10-bai-bien-dep-nhat-chau-a-011.jpg)
_Tourists are strolling along the sandy beach._
![](https://danangfantasticity.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/chiem-nguong-bien-my-khe-1-trong-10-bai-bien-dep-nhat-chau-a-014.jpg)
_The city has created several miniature landscapes for tourists to visit. The rescue team is always on duty to ensure the safety of swimmers._
![](https://danangfantasticity.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/chiem-nguong-bien-my-khe-1-trong-10-bai-bien-dep-nhat-chau-a-015.jpg)
_The rescue team is always on duty to ensure the safety of swimmers._
![](https://danangfantasticity.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/chiem-nguong-bien-my-khe-1-trong-10-bai-bien-dep-nhat-chau-a-016.jpg)
_At My Khe beach, visitors can experience pulling nets with fishermen._
![](https://danangfantasticity.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/chiem-nguong-bien-my-khe-1-trong-10-bai-bien-dep-nhat-chau-a-017.jpg)
_My Khe Beach is a popular destination for marine sports enthusiasts._
