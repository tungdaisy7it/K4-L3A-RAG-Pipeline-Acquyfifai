# Discover My Son with Ease – Ticket Prices, Distance, Transportation & Useful Travel Tips
###### 15/06/2026
Share
Add to Favorites
Print
> Among the many attractions near Da Nang, My Son Sanctuary – a UNESCO World Heritage Site – possesses a unique charm, offering a gentle yet profound experience that lingers long after a single visit.
Far from being noisy or overly commercialized, this thousand-year-old Cham sanctuary presents a journey that combines culture, nature, and emotion, making it an ideal destination for travelers who love discovery.
![](https://danangfantasticity.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/khu-den-thap-my-son-1024x576.jpg)
At My Son, visitors not only admire ancient brick towers standing quietly amid a lush green valley, but also gain insight into a once-flourishing civilization reflected in every architectural detail and surviving bas-relief. It is a place where you can slow down, relax, take beautiful photographs, and learn fascinating stories about the Champa civilization.
My Son offers a “three-in-one” experience: history, art, and nature. Morning sunlight filters through the ancient towers, gentle afternoon breezes sweep across the valley, and traditional Cham performances bring the heritage site to life with emotion and vitality.
For those seeking something truly different, the “Legendary My Son Night” program is one of the most distinctive experiences available. As night falls, carefully designed lighting and sound transform the valley into a magical open-air stage. Cham dancers perform against the backdrop of ancient towers while the rhythms of Paranung drums echo through the mountains, creating a captivating heritage story told through a modern and emotional language.
![](https://danangfantasticity.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/khu-den-thap-my-son-2-1024x576.jpg)
**TICKET PRICES – AFFORDABLE AND ACCESSIBLE**
My Son is one of the most reasonably priced heritage attractions in the region:
  * Vietnamese visitors: VND 100,000 per person
  * International visitors: VND 150,000 per person
  * Children aged 5–15: VND 30,000 per person


The ticket includes access to the My Son temple complex, My Son Museum, electric shuttle service, traditional Cham cultural performances, and visits to archaeological sites throughout the valley.
For visitors wishing to explore the history and architecture of Champa in greater depth, guided interpretation services are available at reasonable rates depending on group size.
**OPENING HOURS**
  * Visiting hours: 06:00 – 18:00
  * Ticket sales: 06:00 – 17:00
  * My Son Museum: 06:00 – 17:00


Morning is considered the ideal time to visit thanks to cooler temperatures, beautiful light, and the tranquil atmosphere of the valley.
![](https://danangfantasticity.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Khu-den-thap-My-Son-7-1024x576.jpg)
**DON’T MISS THE CHAM CULTURAL PERFORMANCES**
One of the most popular experiences at My Son is the daily Cham folk-art performances.
Shows are typically scheduled at 09:00, 09:45, 10:30, and 11:15 in the morning, and at 14:00, 14:45, and 16:00 in the afternoon. Graceful Apsara dances, traditional music, and colorful costumes help visitors better appreciate Cham culture within a heritage landscape over a thousand years old.
On the 15th day of every lunar month, the “Echoes of My Son” art program takes place at the BCD Tower Group, offering a rich and memorable cultural experience.
Traditional Cham folk singing performances are also held regularly on Mondays, Wednesdays, and Fridays.
**DISTANCE AND ACCESSIBILITY – GETTING TO MY SON IS EASY**
Although located in a green valley in My Son Hamlet, Thu Bon Commune, Da Nang City, the sanctuary is easily accessible from major tourism centers:
  * Da Nang city center → My Son: approximately 45–50 km (1 hour 15 minutes to 1 hour 30 minutes)
  * Hoi An → My Son: approximately 35 km (45 minutes to 1 hour)
  * Chu Lai Airport → My Son: approximately 85 km (around 2 hours)

![](https://danangfantasticity.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/khu-den-thap-my-son-4-1024x576.jpg)
The distance is perfect for a short getaway, allowing visitors to leave urban life behind and immerse themselves in the peaceful setting of a World Heritage Site.
**TRANSPORTATION OPTIONS**
Motorbike – Ideal for independent travelers who enjoy adventure and scenic countryside routes.
Private Car or Taxi – Comfortable and convenient for families and groups, with flexibility in timing and weather conditions.
Guided Tour – Perfect for those wishing to gain deeper insights into Cham history, architecture, and heritage stories through professional guides.
![](https://danangfantasticity.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Khu-den-thap-My-Son-0-1024x576.jpg)
**USEFUL TIPS FOR A MORE COMPLETE EXPERIENCE**
  * Visit early, preferably between 06:30 and 09:00, for the best weather and photography conditions.
  * Wear comfortable clothing and suitable footwear, as the site covers a large outdoor area.
  * Bring essentials such as drinking water, sunscreen, a hat, umbrella, or light raincoat depending on the season.
  * Take time to learn about the stories behind each tower group through interpretation services or guided tours.
  * Combine your visit with Hoi An and other nearby attractions for a richer day trip experience.
  * Plan your schedule around the Cham cultural performances to gain a deeper understanding of local traditions.
  * Respect the heritage site by following regulations, avoiding climbing on monuments, refraining from touching artifacts, and keeping the area clean.
  * If possible, experience My Son at night through the “Legendary My Son Night” program, subject to management schedules or pre-arranged group bookings.


> A small tip: My Son is best enjoyed as a half-day or full-day trip. Visitors should allocate at least 3–4 hours to explore the main tower groups, visit the museum, and enjoy Cham cultural performances in order to fully appreciate the site’s heritage values.
With its convenient location, affordable ticket prices, outstanding cultural performances, and a heritage landscape dating back more than a thousand years, My Son is an ideal destination for weekend escapes, cultural journeys, and travelers seeking a unique experience in Central Vietnam.
