# Beautiful beaches that will make you fall in love with Da Nang
###### 18/03/2026
Share
Add to Favorites
Print
> Da Nang offers a sense of ease from the very moment you step onto the beach – where gentle golden sunlight, clear blue skies, and the ocean breeze softly awaken your senses. Without needing to search for anything far, simply standing by the sea allows you to feel a rhythm that is uniquely its own – both relaxing and vibrant, turning every moment into something naturally memorable.
Along its long stretch of coastline, experiences unfold effortlessly and seamlessly – from barefoot walks on soft sand and peaceful seaside strolls in soft light, to moments of simply embracing the openness of waves and wind. It is also the perfect setting to plan your ideal beach moments – whether it’s swimming, trying water sports like SUP, surfing, or jet skiing, or spending an easy afternoon walking along the shore, playing beach volleyball, or enjoying a light picnic with friends.
![](https://danangfantasticity.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Bien-Da-Nang-1-1024x576.jpg)
**Where will you start?**
Stretching along the pristine coastline of Central Vietnam, Da Nang’s beaches have long been internationally recognized as among the most beautiful in Asia. Fine white sand, gently curving shores, crystal-clear waters, and a stunning mountain backdrop create a rare harmony of nature—where every stop offers a distinct atmosphere, inviting you to connect with the sea in your own way.
![](https://danangfantasticity.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Bien-Binh-Minh-1-1024x576.jpg)
From the city center, just 5–10 minutes east along Vo Nguyen Giap – Truong Sa coastal roads, the sea appears right before your eyes. My Khe Beach (An Hai ward) opens up with its long stretch of soft white sand, calm waters, and wide open space. Here, the rhythm of the sea begins early in the morning and lasts until sunset. You can start your day with a swim, a jog, a beach yoga session, or even a gentle morning surf. By afternoon, the atmosphere becomes more lively with beach sports, photo moments, and groups of friends soaking in the energy of the coast.
![](https://danangfantasticity.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Bien-Da-Nang-2-1024x576.jpg)
Heading further south along the coastline, just a few more minutes brings you to My An – An Thuong (Ngu Hanh Son ward). This area has a different vibe—young, open, and full of energy. The sea remains the highlight, but it is seamlessly connected with cafés, restaurants, beach bars, and an international lifestyle. A morning swim followed by brunch, then an afternoon chilling at a beach bar or joining seaside yoga or fitness sessions creates a journey that is both relaxing and dynamic—perfectly aligned with modern beach tourism.
![](https://danangfantasticity.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/HM-2-1024x576.jpg)
Continuing along Truong Sa coastal road for about 30 minutes, the journey extends toward Hoi An ward, where Ha My Beach (Dien Ban ward) and An Bang Beach (Hoi An Tay ward) reveal a softer, more intimate beauty. Without the crowds or noise, these beaches offer a slower rhythm—ideal for lounging on a beanbag, reading a book, enjoying fresh seafood, or simply listening to the waves. Nearby, Cua Dai Beach (Hoi An Tay ward), where the river meets the sea, opens up a spacious landscape perfect for cycling along the coast, exploring riverside routes, and experiencing the blend of nature and heritage.
From Cua Dai, the journey can take a completely different turn—out to the open sea. Just a 15–20 minute speedboat ride away, Cu Lao Cham (Tan Hiep commune) emerges like a separate world, where nature remains remarkably untouched. As a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve, it offers unique experiences: snorkeling among vibrant coral reefs, trekking through forest trails, visiting fishing villages, and enjoying fresh seafood in a peaceful island setting. It’s a chance to connect more deeply with nature—where your journey goes beyond the shoreline and into the ocean.
![](https://danangfantasticity.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Cu-Lao-Cham-1-1024x576.jpg)
Further south, about 40–60 minutes from Da Nang’s center, the coastline takes on another character. Tam Thanh (Quang Phu ward) is not just a beach, but a space of art and fishing village life. You can walk through the mural village, chat with locals, go out to sea with fishermen, or join in everyday activities like net fishing and seafood preparation. Along this coastal route, places like Binh Minh, Tam Hai, and Ban Than reveal even more untouched beauty—perfect for exploration, camping, photography, and truly connecting with nature in its purest form.
![](https://danangfantasticity.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Chieu-Tam-Thanh-Le-Hong-Hanh-1024x693.jpg)
**Don’t miss the most beautiful moments of the day**
Connecting with the sea is not only about the long sandy shores or clear waters—it is most vivid in the early hours of the day. Before sunrise, the sea is already awake. Fishing boats return after a long night, fresh catches are brought ashore, and the lively atmosphere spreads through coastal villages. In Man Thai (Son Tra ward) or southern coastal areas like Tam Hai and Tam Tien, you’ll encounter a very different Da Nang—not of tourism, but of everyday life. Here, the sea is not only beautiful—it is alive, with every moment carrying a genuine rhythm.
![](https://danangfantasticity.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Binh-minh-Cua-Dai-Do-Anh-Vu-1024x683.jpg)
As the sun rises higher, the sea becomes radiant and full of energy. By late afternoon, the light softens, casting a golden glow over the water and creating a different atmosphere—slower, deeper, and quietly inviting you to stay a little longer.
**Sometimes, all it takes is one step toward the sea**
Da Nang right now is not only beautiful—it is in its most ideal state to be experienced. The sea is bluer, the sunlight clearer, and the atmosphere perfectly balanced: lively enough to immerse yourself, yet gentle enough to feel every moment in your own way.
![](https://danangfantasticity.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Bien-Binh-Minh-4-1024x683.jpg) ![](https://danangfantasticity.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Bien-Binh-Minh-3-1024x576.jpg)
It could be a morning swim in cool waters, an afternoon drifting with the sunset, or a longer journey along the coastline to discover untouched landscapes and local life. Each choice opens up a different way to connect—with nature, with the rhythm of life, and with yourself.
And sometimes, you don’t need to plan too much. Just follow the sun, the wind, and the sound of the waves—Da Nang will naturally lead you to its most memorable experiences.
