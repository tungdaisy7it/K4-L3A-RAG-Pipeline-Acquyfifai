# Summer 2026: Open the Gates to Ba Na and Discover Four Magical Worlds
###### 15/06/2026
Share
Add to Favorites
Print
> _This summer, Sun World Ba Na Hills invites visitors on an inspiring journey through four unique lands, four magical worlds, and hundreds of exciting new experiences waiting to be discovered._
![](https://danangfantasticity.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/he-nay-mo-canh-cong-den-ba-na-va-kham-pha-4-the-gioi-dieu-ky-1024x576.jpg)
Behind each gate lies a completely different world, where visitors can enjoy distinctive moments and create unforgettable memories with family and friends.
![](https://danangfantasticity.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/he-nay-mo-canh-cong-den-ba-na-va-kham-pha-4-the-gioi-dieu-ky-2.png) ![](https://danangfantasticity.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/he-nay-mo-canh-cong-den-ba-na-va-kham-pha-4-the-gioi-dieu-ky3.jpg)
### **The Sun Kingdom – A Vibrant Festival Atmosphere**
The first stop on the journey is the Sun Kingdom, a land filled with dazzling colors and a lively festival ambiance. Spectacular performances and vibrant surroundings create an uplifting energy, bringing joy and excitement throughout the adventure.
### **The Fairyland – A World of Blossoming Beauty**
Step through the next gate and enter the Fairyland, where colorful blooms transform the landscape into a picture-perfect wonderland. This enchanting area is also an ideal place to capture memorable moments, with countless stunning photo opportunities waiting around every corner.
![](https://danangfantasticity.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/he-nay-mo-canh-cong-den-ba-na-va-kham-pha-4-the-gioi-dieu-ky-4.png) ![](https://danangfantasticity.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/he-nay-mo-canh-cong-den-ba-na-va-kham-pha-4-the-gioi-dieu-ky-5.png)
### **The Land of Origins – Touch the Clouds**
The Land of Origins offers a different kind of experience, bringing visitors closer to the natural beauty of Ba Na’s mountains and forests. Here, guests can reach the clouds and embrace a sense of tranquility amidst breathtaking mountain scenery and fresh highland air.
### **The Moon Kingdom – A Journey into Mysteries**
The adventure concludes in the Moon Kingdom, where mysterious wonders await discovery among the majestic mountains of Ba Na. This unique realm offers fascinating experiences and adds a sense of wonder and imagination to every family adventure.
### **Explore at Your Own Pace with the “One Ticket, Three Days of Adventure” Pass**
To make the journey even more enjoyable, visitors can leisurely explore all four magical worlds with the special “One Ticket, Three Days of Adventure” pass. Spend more time discovering, experiencing, and creating unforgettable memories throughout your stay at Ba Na Hills.
![](https://danangfantasticity.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/he-nay-mo-canh-cong-den-ba-na-va-kham-pha-4-the-gioi-dieu-ky-003-1024x683.jpg) ![](https://danangfantasticity.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/he-nay-mo-canh-cong-den-ba-na-va-kham-pha-4-the-gioi-dieu-ky-001-1024x683.jpg) ![](https://danangfantasticity.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/he-nay-mo-canh-cong-den-ba-na-va-kham-pha-4-the-gioi-dieu-ky-002-1024x683.jpg)
Please note that one-day tickets must be upgraded to the three-day pass before the first use to enjoy the full benefits of the program.
From January 1 to December 31, 2026, a single Ba Na Hills ticket allows access to the cable car system for three consecutive days, giving visitors extra time to fully immerse themselves in the experiences and attractions of Sun World Ba Na Hills.
This summer, open the gates to Ba Na and begin your journey through four magical worlds.
